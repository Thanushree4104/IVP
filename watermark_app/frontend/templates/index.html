Three Embedding Methods

Choose the right technique for your use case

DCT

Discrete Cosine Transform

Block-DCT spread-spectrum. Splits image into 8×8 blocks and modifies mid-frequency coefficients. JPEG-resistant and fast.

JPEG resistant Block-based Fast
DWT

Discrete Wavelet Transform

Single-level Haar wavelet decomposition. Embeds in the LH sub-band for multi-resolution robustness across scales.

Multi-resolution Haar wavelet LH band
HYBRID

DWT + DCT Combined

DWT decomposes the image, then DCT is applied to the LH sub-band. Strongest method combining both transforms.

Strongest Dual-domain Recommended

How It Works

End-to-end watermarking pipeline

1

Set Your Password

Enter a secret AES-256 password. This derives the encryption key via PBKDF2 — the same password is required to verify.

2

Upload Your Image

Upload the cover image you want to protect. PNG and JPEG formats are supported.

3

Choose Watermark Type

Enter a text watermark (copyright notice, author info) or upload a binary logo to embed as your mark.

4

Select Method & Embed

Pick DCT, DWT, or Hybrid (recommended). The watermark is AES encrypted, Reed-Solomon protected, then frequency-domain embedded.

5

Download & Verify

Download the watermarked image. Later, upload it to the Verify tab with your password to prove ownership.
